Himalayan Buzz: From Digital Content to Live Events

Himalayan Buzz (official Instagram) is an event and media company running major events across Dehradun and Uttarakhand. This client relationship pushed Redsan beyond screens and into physical event production.

The scope of work included:

  • › Creating content for the Himalayan Buzz website and social media channels
  • › Producing stage-based visual and video content for live events
  • › Managing multiple high-frequency social media accounts simultaneously during live proceedings

Events Redsan Actively Worked On

Redsan was directly involved in the production and promotion of several major Uttarakhand events, including:

  • › Miss Uttarakhand 2025 and Mr Uttarakhand 2025: Covering the full event cycle, including the winners meeting ADG Amit Sinha and visiting the UNHU Startup Emporium in Dehradun.
  • › Dehradun Food Awards 2025: Honouring the best in the city's food scene, celebrating top regional culinary founders, chefs, and hospitality pioneers.
  • › Uttarakhand Wedding Awards 2025: Highlighting Uttarakhand's leading event designers, wedding venues, and experiential planners.
  • › Uttarakhand Education Awards 2025: Recognizing academic excellence across prominent universities, schools, and educational institutes.
  • › Dehradun Food Awards 2026: Honouring the city's finest culinary talent, alongside the Mr & Miss Uttarakhand 2026 crowning.

Redsan's founder personally attended these events to ensure media coverage and promotion were being handled to the standard the client needed, hands-on presence rather than remote content management.

A Door Into Dehradun's Startup Ecosystem: UNHU

One unexpected outcome of the Himalayan Buzz relationship was getting connected to UNHU, Dehradun's own startup ecosystem, through the Miss/Mr Uttarakhand 2025 visit to the UNHU Startup Emporium. UNHU runs a founder network of 1,000-plus entrepreneurs in Dehradun, and its Startup Emporium functions as a physical retail space where early-stage brands test products and build visibility directly in front of real customers. Being present at these events put Redsan in the room with a genuinely active local entrepreneur community, not just event attendees, opening up relationships with founders and businesses well beyond the immediate scope of the Himalayan Buzz contract.

Getting Credited in Print

Redsan also worked on producing the Himalayan Buzz magazine for these events. In these publications, both Redsan's founder Sankalp Reddy and Animesh, a prominent member of the Redsan team, are directly credited by name, real print recognition for work most agencies only ever describe in a portfolio deck.

50+ Businesses, One Growing Network

Working alongside Himalayan Buzz to pull off these events gave Redsan the chance to closely work with more than 50 businesses within the food and beverage industry and adjacent sectors, producing videos, posts, and building genuine networking relationships across Dehradun's business community. All of that work was in service of running these events successfully alongside Himalayan Buzz, and it ended up being one of the biggest single factors in Redsan establishing itself in the Dehradun market. Himalayan Buzz became one of Redsan's major clients, and this relationship is a big part of how Redsan built real presence in this city.

Why This Chapter Matters

Being physically in the room at Uttarakhand's biggest public events, getting named in print for it, and building a network of 50-plus business relationships in the process, isn't something an agency can manufacture through content alone. This chapter is where Redsan moved from being a service vendor to being a recognized part of how these events actually get built.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of work did Redsan do for Himalayan Buzz?

Redsan managed website and social media content, produced stage-based content for live events, and actively worked on major Uttarakhand events including Miss/Mr Uttarakhand, the Dehradun Food Awards, the Uttarakhand Wedding Awards, and the Uttarakhand Education Awards.

Did Redsan's team get public recognition for this work?

Yes. Redsan's founder Sankalp Reddy and team member Animesh are directly credited by name in the Himalayan Buzz event magazines.

How significant was the Himalayan Buzz relationship for Redsan's growth?

Very. Beyond direct client work, running events alongside Himalayan Buzz connected Redsan with more than 50 businesses in the food and beverage industry and related sectors, building relationships and market presence that helped establish the agency in Dehradun.

Was Redsan's founder personally involved in these events?

Yes. Redsan's founder personally attended these events to ensure media coverage and promotion were handled to the standard the client needed, focusing on hands-on presence rather than remote management.

Did this relationship connect Redsan to Dehradun's startup community?

Yes. Through the Miss/Mr Uttarakhand 2025 visit to the UNHU Startup Emporium, Redsan got connected to UNHU, Dehradun's startup ecosystem with a 1,000-plus founder network, opening relationships with entrepreneurs beyond the immediate Himalayan Buzz contract.
