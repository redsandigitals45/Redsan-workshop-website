# Beyond Dehradun: Redsan's Pan-India Growth Stories

## Quick Answer

Dehradun is where Redsan built its foundation, but geography has never defined the kind of brands Redsan can work with. This chapter documents three very different outcomes delivered for businesses across India: a generator rental business that moved from an average SERP position of 106 to 12, a culture and travel content brand that grew beyond 110,000 Instagram followers alongside an e-commerce and paid-acquisition build, and an Ayurveda manufacturing brand that crossed ₹50 lakh in turnover while Redsan built its social presence and ran UGC and influencer campaigns.

Together, these projects show how Redsan's full-stack growth capabilities can support both **B2B and D2C brands** across acquisition, content, SEO, e-commerce, performance marketing and social growth.

---

## 01 — Max Power Solutions: Turning Search Visibility into B2B Growth

[Max Power Solutions](https://maxpowersolution.co.in/blog.html) is a generator rental services business and one of the clearest SEO examples in Redsan's portfolio.

For a B2B business, visibility on Google is not simply about traffic. It is about being discoverable when a potential customer is actively looking for a solution.

### The work

The engagement covered:

- Managing the business's [social media presence](https://www.instagram.com/max.power.solutions/) with authoritative, industry-relevant posts
- Fixing on-page SEO fundamentals and writing SEO-friendly blog content
- Building off-page SEO backlinks and working through local business listings
- Managing and actively updating the Google Business Profile
- Writing and publishing ongoing blog articles

### The measurable outcome

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| On-page SEO score | 40 | **95** |
| Average SERP ranking | 106 | **12** |
| Organic CTR | 0.2% | **1.4%** |

Redsan retained this client for a full year. Moving from position 106 to position 12 transformed the business's search visibility from effectively difficult to discover into a position where relevant prospects could genuinely find it.

**Why it matters for B2B:** this is an example of building an acquisition channel around search intent rather than relying entirely on outbound sales or paid advertising.

## 02 — Somarasa: Building a 110K+ Audience and the E-Commerce Engine Behind It

[Somarasa Discoveries](https://www.instagram.com/somarasa_/) describes itself as a brand exploring where tradition meets transformation, spotlighting culture and traditions that don't usually get mainstream attention.

Redsan's work here moved beyond social media into the infrastructure required to turn attention into commerce, supporting [The Soma Store](https://thesomastore.com/), the brand's e-commerce arm.

### The work

The scope included:

- Creating content and handling social media across the brand's profiles
- Optimizing the website for on-page SEO and actively monitoring performance through Google Search Console
- Setting up e-commerce infrastructure, including product listings
- Running paid acquisition through both Meta and Google Ads
- Producing UGC-style ad creative

### The outcome

The brand's Instagram page has grown to more than **110,000 followers**.

The significance is not the follower number alone. The project demonstrates how content and audience building can connect with the systems required to convert attention into online commerce:

**Content → Audience → E-Commerce → Paid Acquisition → Conversion**

**Why it matters for D2C:** scaling an online consumer brand requires more than posting consistently. The audience, storefront, search visibility, creative and acquisition engine need to work together.

## 03 — Bhagat Ayurveda: Building a Social Presence Around a Growing Business

[Bhagat Ayurveda Manufacturing](https://www.instagram.com/bhagat_ayurveda_manufacturing/) is where Redsan's work moved into direct business-growth territory for a manufacturing brand.

Redsan built the brand's social presence from the ground up and worked across content, UGC-style advertising and influencer marketing.

### The work

- Building and managing the brand's social media presence from scratch
- Producing posts, Reels and UGC-style ads
- Running influencer marketing campaigns

### The outcome

The page has grown to more than **4,000 followers**, and the business has crossed **₹50 lakh in turnover**.

The turnover figure is the more important business metric; the follower count represents the visible layer of the broader digital presence Redsan helped build.

To keep attribution precise, the chapter does not claim that the entire ₹50 lakh turnover came from Redsan's campaigns. Instead, it documents the business outcome alongside the scope of Redsan's engagement.

**Why it matters for B2B and D2C:** manufacturers and product businesses need a digital presence that can support both brand building and demand generation. Social content, UGC and influencer distribution can become part of that system when connected to a broader commercial strategy.
