Beyond Dehradun: Redsan's Pan-India Growth Stories

SR
Sankalp Reddy
15 Sep 2026 · 6 min read
Quick Answer: Dehradun is where Redsan built its foundation, but geography has never defined the kind of brands Redsan can work with. This chapter documents three very different outcomes delivered for businesses across India: a generator rental business that moved from an average SERP position of 106 to 12, a culture and travel content brand that grew beyond 110,000 Instagram followers alongside an e-commerce and paid-acquisition build, and an Ayurveda manufacturing brand that crossed ₹50 lakh in turnover while Redsan built its social presence and ran UGC and influencer campaigns.

Together, these projects show how Redsan's full-stack growth capabilities can support both B2B and D2C brands across acquisition, content, SEO, e-commerce, performance marketing, and social growth.

01: Max Power Solutions: Turning Search Visibility into B2B Growth

Max Power Solutions is a generator rental services business and one of the clearest SEO examples in Redsan's portfolio.

For a B2B business, visibility on Google is not simply about traffic. It is about being discoverable when a potential customer is actively looking for an industrial or commercial rental solution.

Max Power Solutions Engagement

B2B Search Visibility
The Work Delivered:
  • Managing the business's social media presence with authoritative, industry-relevant posts
  • Fixing on-page SEO fundamentals and writing SEO-friendly blog content
  • Building off-page SEO backlinks and working through local business listings
  • Managing and actively updating the Google Business Profile
  • Writing and publishing ongoing technical blog articles
The Measurable Outcome:
Key Metric Before Redsan After Redsan
On-page SEO score 40 95
Average SERP ranking 106 12
Organic Click-Through Rate (CTR) 0.2% 1.4%

Redsan retained this client for a full year. Moving from position 106 to position 12 transformed the business's search visibility from effectively difficult to discover into a position where relevant prospects could genuinely find it.

Why it matters for B2B: This is an example of building an acquisition channel around search intent rather than relying entirely on outbound sales or paid advertising.

02: Somarasa: Building a 110K+ Audience and the E-Commerce Engine Behind It

Somarasa Discoveries describes itself as a brand exploring where tradition meets transformation, spotlighting culture and traditions that do not usually get mainstream attention.

Redsan's work here moved beyond social media into the infrastructure required to turn attention into commerce, supporting The Soma Store, the brand's e-commerce arm.

Somarasa Discoveries & The Soma Store

D2C Audience & Commerce
The Scope Included:
  • Creating content and handling social media across the brand's profiles
  • Optimizing the website for on-page SEO and actively monitoring performance through Google Search Console
  • Setting up e-commerce infrastructure, including product listings
  • Running paid acquisition through both Meta and Google Ads
  • Producing UGC-style ad creative for conversion campaigns
The Outcome:

The brand's Instagram page has grown to more than 110,000 followers.

The significance is not the follower number alone. The project demonstrates how content and audience building connect directly with the systems required to convert attention into online commerce.

Content → Audience → E-Commerce → Paid Acquisition → Conversion

Why it matters for D2C: Scaling an online consumer brand requires more than posting consistently. The audience, storefront, search visibility, creative, and acquisition engine need to work together as a unified system.

03: Bhagat Ayurveda: Building a Social Presence Around a Growing Business

Bhagat Ayurveda Manufacturing is where Redsan's work moved into direct business-growth territory for a manufacturing brand.

Redsan built the brand's social presence from the ground up and worked across content, UGC-style advertising, and influencer marketing.

Bhagat Ayurveda Manufacturing

Manufacturing & D2C
The Work:
  • Building and managing the brand's social media presence from scratch
  • Producing high-velocity posts, Reels, and UGC-style ads
  • Running targeted influencer marketing campaigns
The Outcome:

The page has grown to more than 4,000 followers, and the business has crossed ₹50 lakh in turnover.

The turnover figure is the primary commercial metric; the follower count represents the visible layer of the broader digital presence Redsan helped build. To maintain precise attribution standards, this record documents the overall business outcome achieved alongside the scope of Redsan's engagement.

Why it matters for B2B and D2C: Manufacturers and product businesses need a digital presence capable of supporting both brand building and demand generation. Social content, UGC, and influencer distribution become powerful parts of that system when connected to a broader commercial strategy.

What These Projects Actually Prove

These three engagements represent three completely different business models:

  • B2B rental services: Search visibility and high-intent local discovery
  • D2C culture and commerce: Audience building, e-commerce storefront, and paid acquisition
  • Manufacturing / product business: Social presence, UGC, and influencer distribution

And they produced three distinct kinds of measurable outcomes:

That range is critical. Redsan does not approach every business with the same channel or the same playbook. The objective is to engineer the growth system around the business model, the customer journey, and the underlying commercial opportunity.

Why This Chapter Matters

Dehradun was Redsan's base. It was never its boundary.

The work documented in this chapter shows Redsan delivering for businesses beyond Dehradun and Uttarakhand while resolving distinct growth bottlenecks.

More importantly, it shows the direction of the company:

From individual marketing services → to connected growth systems.

For a B2B company, that can mean building search visibility, industry authority, and inbound demand.

For a D2C brand, it means connecting content, audience growth, e-commerce infrastructure, paid acquisition, and conversion.

The channels change. The underlying principle does not:

Build the engine behind the growth.

That is the role Redsan is building toward: helping B2B and D2C brands scale online through a connected full-stack approach to content, SEO, performance marketing, e-commerce, social media, and digital infrastructure.

From Local Roots to Scalable Growth

The earlier Redsan chapters document how the company built relationships, entered new ecosystems, and developed its capabilities. This chapter adds another piece to that story:

Redsan can be rooted in Dehradun while building for markets across India.

That distinction matters for ambitious brands. A company does not need its growth partner to be based in the same city. It needs a partner capable of understanding its business, building the right digital systems, and executing consistently across the customer journey.

That is where Redsan's full-stack model is designed to operate:

B2B Customer Journey
Search → Authority → Content → Lead Generation → Conversion
D2C Customer Journey
Content → Audience → E-Commerce → Paid Acquisition → Conversion

Different businesses. Different journeys. One connected growth philosophy.
