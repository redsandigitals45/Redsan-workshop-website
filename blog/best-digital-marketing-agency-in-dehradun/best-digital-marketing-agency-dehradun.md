## How to Compare Any Agency, Including the Ones Above

### 1. Relevant Experience

Check whether the agency has worked with businesses similar to yours: local services, hospitality and tourism, education, healthcare, real estate, e-commerce, B2B, or restaurants. Experience in one sector doesn't automatically transfer to another.

### 2. Service Fit

| Business goal | Relevant services |
|---|---|
| Improve organic visibility | Technical SEO, local SEO, content, link building |
| Generate immediate leads | Google Ads, landing pages, conversion tracking |
| Build local reputation | Google Business Profile, local SEO, reviews |
| Sell products online | E-commerce SEO, shopping ads, Meta Ads, CRO |
| Build brand awareness | Social media, video, creative, influencer marketing |
| Improve website performance | Web design, UX, speed optimization, analytics |

A full-service agency isn't automatically the right choice. A specialist can be a better fit for technical SEO, performance advertising, or e-commerce-specific growth.

### 3. Proof of Results

Ask for before-and-after traffic data, cost per lead, search visibility improvements, ad account screenshots, and client references, with a clear measurement period attached. Follower growth or impressions alone aren't proof of business growth unless tied to leads, sales, or bookings.

### 4. Reporting and Ownership

Before signing, confirm you'll own or have access to Google Analytics, Search Console, your ad accounts, hosting and domain, and creative files. An agency shouldn't hold your essential accounts hostage, access and exit terms should be clear in the agreement.

### 5. Local Understanding

A Dehradun-based agency can bring real value for local search, tourism and hospitality, coaching institutes, and local retail, but location alone isn't proof of quality. Strategy, technical capability, and measurable results still matter more.

## FAQs

**Q1: Which is the best digital marketing agency in Dehradun?**
There is no universally best agency for every business. The right choice depends on whether you need SEO, Google Ads, social media, web development, or e-commerce support, compare agencies on relevant experience, verifiable case studies, and reporting transparency rather than star ratings alone.

**Q2: How much does a digital marketing agency charge in Dehradun?**
Indicative listings show starter packages around ₹10,000–₹20,000 per month, with broader SEO or full-service retainers often in the ₹25,000–₹50,000+ per month range. Ad spend and taxes are usually billed separately.

**Q3: Do I need a Dehradun-based agency specifically?**
Not always. A local agency can bring genuine value through local search knowledge, face-to-face communication, and local business networks, but expertise and proven results matter more than location alone.

**Q4: How can I verify an agency's claims before signing?**
Ask for case studies with named metrics and measurement periods, client references, and confirmation that you'll retain access to your own Analytics, Search Console, and ad accounts throughout the engagement.
