# Before Redsan: The International Client Track Record Behind Our Founder's Experience

## Quick Answer

Before founding Redsan Digitals, Sankalp Reddy spent time working as a Digital Marketing Executive and Executive Assistant to the Director at Firewind Tech, a USA-based digital marketing company, starting in 2019. Over that period, he managed social media, content, digital PR, and creative production for clients across Texas, London, and the wider US and UK market, including a transportation company, a CBD products brand, a Michelin-star chef, and a barbecue restaurant chain. That hands-on, client-facing international experience is part of what shapes how Redsan operates today.

---

## Where It Started: Firewind Tech, 2019

In 2019, working as a Digital Marketing Executive and Executive Assistant to the Director at [Firewind Tech](https://firewindtech.com/), a USA-based digital marketing company, the role covered clients based across America and London. The work spanned writing website content, building out a team, hiring, and managing client projects directly, hands-on execution work rather than strategy from a distance.

Four client engagements from this period stand out as a clear record of what that experience actually involved.

---

## Mainstay USA: Transportation Company Social Media

[Mainstay USA](https://www.instagram.com/mainstayusa/) was a transportation company client handled during this period. The scope of work included:

- Planning social media content
- Writing creative copy
- Learning Canva to produce social media creatives from scratch
- Creating client briefing decks and presentation materials
- Producing short edited promotional videos
- Managing the account through Meta Business Suite alongside Buffer, a social media management and scheduling tool
- Managing Yelp reviews

Over this engagement, more than 45 creatives and 10-plus videos were produced for the brand. The Mainstay USA page has since grown to over 4,500 followers.

---

## Artisan Vapor: Multi-Location CBD Retail Brand

[Artisan Vapor Company](https://www.artisanvaporcompany.com/landing/), a CBD products and oil company operating more than five retail stores across Texas, USA, was another client managed during this period. The scope of work included:

- Managing multiple social media profiles simultaneously through Buffer
- Creating creatives and videos using Canva and CapCut
- Handling inventory management alongside marketing responsibilities
- Designing and writing content while maintaining consistent branding and product asset standards
- Working directly with the client's team on their website build
- Managing Yelp reviews across multiple store locations

Running marketing for a multi-location retail brand meant coordinating consistent branding across five-plus physical stores at once, not just a single social feed.

---

## Chandan Taneja: Michelin-Star Chef, London

[Chandan Taneja](https://www.chandantaneja.com/) is a renowned Michelin-star chef based in London, UK. For this client, the work went beyond standard social media management into full digital presence building and digital PR:

- Created his Instagram and LinkedIn profiles from the ground up
- Built and managed his website
- Managed digital PR, including securing coverage in major newspaper publications, such as [The Hindu's feature on his culinary story](https://www.thehindu.com/features/metroplus/Food/finding-his-heart-in-food/article6111814.ece)
- Planned, created, and posted content across multiple platforms
- Handled core social media management and digital PR strategy end to end

This engagement was less about running ads and more about building an entire digital identity and media presence for a high-profile individual client, from zero.

---

## L&L Hawaiian BBQ: Restaurant Marketing, Arlington, Texas

[L&L Hawaiian BBQ](https://www.hawaiianbarbecue.com/) is a barbecue restaurant based in Arlington, Texas, USA. The scope of work here included:

- Writing website content
- Managing and handling the restaurant's social media profiles
- Managing Yelp reviews for the location

For a local restaurant business, the work centered on the fundamentals that drive real foot traffic and reputation, consistent content, active social presence, and review management.
