Before Redsan: The International Client Track Record Behind Our Founder's Experience

SR
Sankalp Reddy
12 Sep 2026 · 5 min read
Quick Answer: Before founding Redsan Digitals, Sankalp Reddy spent time working as a Digital Marketing Executive and Executive Assistant to the Director at Firewind Tech, a USA-based digital marketing company, starting in 2019. Over that period, he managed social media, content, digital PR, and creative production for clients across Texas, London, and the wider US and UK market, including a transportation company, a CBD products brand, a Michelin-star chef, and a barbecue restaurant chain. That hands-on, client-facing international experience is part of what shapes how Redsan operates today.

A common critique of digital marketing agencies, particularly those operating outside of major metropolitan tier-1 hubs, is that claims of expertise often dissolve under scrutiny. Too many agencies sell strategic advisory without ever having handled real ad spend, real multi-location brand standards, or discerning international client stakeholders.

The foundation behind Redsan Digitals was built on direct, client-facing execution across highly demanding US and UK markets. Here is the verifiable record of that work and why it directly influences our agency delivery model today.

Where It Started: Firewind Tech, 2019

In 2019, working as a Digital Marketing Executive and Executive Assistant to the Director at Firewind Tech, a USA-based digital marketing company, the role covered clients based across America and London. The work spanned writing website content, building out a team, hiring, and managing client projects directly: hands-on execution work rather than strategy from a distance.

Firewind Tech

Agency Operations · USA & UK

Role: Digital Marketing Executive & Executive Assistant to Director. Direct management of multi-client workflows, team hiring, content production pipelines, and commercial deliverables across international time zones.

Four client engagements from this period stand out as a clear record of what that experience actually involved.

Mainstay USA: Transportation Company Social Media

Mainstay USA was a transportation company client handled during this period. Operating in a high-trust commercial logistics market required precise messaging and disciplined visual communication.

Mainstay USA

Transportation & Logistics · United States
Scope of Execution:
  • Planning monthly social media content calendars tailored to B2B and consumer transportation audiences
  • Writing creative copy for promotions, service announcements, and fleet highlights
  • Learning Canva to produce high-impact social media creatives from scratch
  • Creating client briefing decks and visual presentation materials for executive review
  • Producing short edited promotional videos for multi-channel distribution
  • Managing account operations through Meta Business Suite alongside Buffer for scheduled posting
  • Monitoring customer sentiment and managing Yelp reviews to preserve organic reputation
Deliverables & Traction: Over 45 original creatives and 10-plus promotional videos produced. The Mainstay USA social channel has since scaled to over 4,500 followers.

Artisan Vapor: Multi-Location CBD Retail Brand

Artisan Vapor Company, a CBD products and oil company operating more than five retail stores across Texas, USA, was another client managed during this period.

Artisan Vapor Company

Retail & CBD Consumer Goods · Texas, USA
Scope of Execution:
  • Managing multiple social media profiles simultaneously across retail branches through Buffer
  • Creating high-engagement creatives and video assets using Canva and CapCut
  • Handling inventory management and digital product listings alongside daily marketing responsibilities
  • Designing and writing compliant content while maintaining strict branding and product asset standards
  • Working directly with the client's engineering team on their primary website build
  • Managing Yelp customer reviews and local store listings across multiple physical retail locations
Operational Moat: Coordinated brand consistency and localized retail campaigns across 5-plus physical storefronts simultaneously, balancing regulatory compliance with customer engagement.

Chandan Taneja: Michelin-Star Chef, London

Chandan Taneja is a renowned Michelin-star chef based in London, UK. For this high-profile culinary brand, the mandate extended far beyond standard social media management into full digital presence building and digital PR.

Chef Chandan Taneja

Fine Dining & Culinary PR · London, UK
Scope of Execution:
  • Created his official Instagram and LinkedIn profiles from the ground up
  • Engineered and managed his personal website portfolio and editorial presence
  • Managed digital PR, securing major press publication coverage including The Hindu's feature on his culinary journey: "Finding His Heart In Food"
  • Planned, produced, and published multimedia culinary storytelling across platforms
  • Handled personal branding, VIP collaborations, and digital PR strategy end to end
Strategic Value: Built an entire digital identity, media reputation, and editorial PR footprint for a high-profile Michelin-star personality from zero.

L&L Hawaiian BBQ: Restaurant Marketing, Arlington, Texas

L&L Hawaiian BBQ is a barbecue restaurant based in Arlington, Texas, USA. For local hospitality businesses, success depends directly on community trust, foot-traffic activation, and review sentiment.

L&L Hawaiian BBQ

Hospitality & Quick Service · Arlington, Texas
Scope of Execution:
  • Writing high-converting website content and seasonal promotional copy
  • Managing and scheduling localized restaurant social media profiles
  • Proactively managing customer reviews on Yelp and Google to protect restaurant reputation
Local Business Fundamentals: Focused on the bedrock drivers of restaurant revenue: active local social proof, responsive customer review cycles, and consistent menu storytelling.

Why This Matters When Choosing a Digital Marketing Partner

A lot of agencies talk about experience in general terms. This is the specific, named, verifiable record behind Redsan's founder: real clients, real industries (transportation, retail CBD, high-end hospitality, restaurant), real markets (Texas, London), and real deliverables, not just claims.

When evaluating a digital marketing agency, especially one based outside a major metro, this kind of documented international client history is worth asking every agency to show, not just tell. It proves that our team understands cross-cultural audience dynamics, international communication standards, and multi-channel marketing rigour.
