# The biggest issue: attribution around 450+ admissions

This is the one area I would be careful with.

The article says Redsan handled extensive digital activity and then says:

> “In the most recent admission season, Maya Devi University closed with more than 450 admissions…”

The reader can easily interpret this as:

**Redsan generated 450 admissions.**

But your current wording doesn't establish that Redsan directly caused all 450 admissions.

That's especially important because you're building a premium, evidence-driven agency brand.

I'd change it to something like:

> **“During the most recent admission season, Maya Devi University closed with more than 450 admissions across its programs while Redsan was managing the university's digital activity.”**

That's factual without overclaiming causality.

Then, if you actually have tracked lead/admission attribution, you can make the claim much stronger later.
