From Robotics to Universities: Redsan's Journey Through the Education Industry

SR
Sankalp Reddy
15 Sep 2026 · 7 min read
Quick Answer: Redsan's work in the education sector did not start as a marketing pitch: it evolved from within the education ecosystem itself. Beginning in 2023 with a robotics education startup and classroom teaching at Social Baluni Public School, Redsan's footprint expanded to student entrepreneurship initiatives with EDC India, guest lectures at premier institutions, and full-stack digital marketing for leading universities. Most notably, Redsan managed the digital activity for Maya Devi University during an admission season closing with 450+ admissions while scaling their social following by 2.5x and impressions to 700K+. This deep institutional immersion also led to the founder being invited to teach entrepreneurship at Chandigarh University and eventually gave birth to The Redsan Workshop.

Education is not a sector most digital marketing agencies build real depth in. It requires navigating lengthy institutional approval cycles, understanding academic compliance, producing high volumes of creative assets across diverse departments, and connecting student culture with measurable admission outcomes.

Redsan's positioning in education is fundamentally different because Redsan did not simply pitch marketing services to academic institutions from the outside: our team operated directly inside the academic and student ecosystem.

Phase 01: The Robotics Genesis at Social Baluni Public School

Redsan's entry into the education space began in 2023 with Ikkashin, an innovative robotics education startup. Rather than operating as an abstract technology provider, the work was deeply rooted in direct classroom delivery.

At Social Baluni Public School, Redsan delivered hands-on robotics education directly to school students. The engagement covered curriculum execution, interactive STEM workshops, and classroom training designed to make complex engineering principles accessible to young learners.

Field Documentation: Robotics curriculum delivery at Social Baluni Public School (2023).

Direct Classroom Teaching

Standing in front of young students and teaching technical concepts provided our team with foundational insights into educational communication, student engagement mechanics, and parent expectations.

Foundation: Hands-on STEM education and school curriculum integration.

Phase 02: Youth Entrepreneurship with EDC India & Build Up Bharat

As Redsan expanded in Dehradun, our institutional footprint connected with EDC India (Entrepreneurship Development Cell), a premier non-profit organization dedicated to fostering startup culture, venture building, and innovation among college students across North India.

Redsan supported EDC India across creative design, youth marketing, and event digital media, culminating in the flagship Build Up Bharat initiative. This exposed the agency to high-energy student communities, institutional stakeholders, government delegates, and young startup founders.

Institutional Footprint: Build Up Bharat initiative executed in partnership with EDC India.

Ecosystem Building

Working alongside EDC India solidified Redsan's understanding of how young aspirational audiences consume content, what triggers peer-to-peer distribution, and how institutional events generate sustained brand authority.

Deliverable: Youth event positioning, graphic design systems, and digital distribution.

Phase 03: The Commercial Centerpiece: Maya Devi University Growth Breakdown

Maya Devi University is the strongest commercial proof point in Redsan's higher education portfolio. Operating as a multidisciplinary university in Dehradun, the institution required a complete digital transformation across social media branding, creative throughput, event coverage, and admission support.

Phase 04: Academic Invitations: Teaching Entrepreneurship at Chandigarh University

Redsan's involvement in education has extended directly to student teaching at premier national institutions. Sankalp Reddy, founder of Redsan, was invited by Chandigarh University, one of India's largest and highest-ranked private universities, to address university students and teach practical startup entrepreneurship.

Rather than presenting generic academic theory, the sessions focused on ground-level agency realities: discovering client demand, pricing services, sales closing, building operational standard operating procedures (SOPs), and leveraging modern AI tools for business execution.

Chandigarh University: Founder addressing university students on practical entrepreneurship.
Interactive Masterclass: Bridging university classroom learning with real commercial execution.

This academic invitation demonstrates a critical differentiator: our leadership has stood on stage inside university lecture halls, connecting with students directly. When Redsan designs campaigns for universities, we understand the student mindset because we interact with them in their own auditoriums.

Phase 05: University Ecosystems: Uttaranchal University & DBS Global University

Redsan's university network in Uttarakhand spans two other prominent institutions: Uttaranchal University and DBS Global University (Doon Business School).

At Uttaranchal University, where Redsan was originally incubated at the university's Startup Emporium, our team delivered guest lectures, conducted practical workshops, and engaged directly with budding university founders.

Uttaranchal University: Founder addressing university students and faculty during an on-campus session.
Startup Ecosystem: Engaging with incubator teams, faculty, and student innovators.
Interactive Lecture: Delivering practical agency playbooks to university classrooms.
Campus Presence: Real-time dialogue on digital marketing careers and agency building.

Similarly, Redsan's relationship with DBS Global University has encompassed academic guest lectures, faculty interactions, and ongoing technical SEO work, helping position the university's academic offerings for high-intent organic search discovery.

From Working in Education to Teaching It: The Redsan Workshop

Redsan's experience in education eventually moved beyond client services. Having worked directly with schools, universities, edtech founders, and student non-profits, our team recognized a persistent gap between academic marketing curriculums and real industry practice.

That realization led to the creation of The Redsan Workshop: our flagship intensive 2-day live workshop covering Digital Marketing with AI, Entrepreneurship, and Sales Closing. Conducted across premier colleges and universities, the workshop brings the exact systems Redsan uses to manage enterprise clients directly into student classrooms.

The Full Circle: We did not become a workshop company that decided to offer marketing. We are an active, high-volume growth agency whose institutional immersion naturally evolved into practical student education.
