What Proof Should an SEO Company Show You?

Before committing budget, require verifiable proof of past performance rather than promotional claims alone.

Essential Proof Points to Request

  • Google Search Console performance: Organic click growth, impression curves, and average position trends over a 6 to 12 month window
  • Google Analytics conversion trends: Growth in actual conversion events (form fills, calls, bookings), not just session counts
  • Qualified leads, not raw inquiries: Evidence that campaigns generated leads matching your actual target customer, not bot traffic
  • Before-and-after case studies: A clear starting position, the work completed, the timeframe, and the final measured outcome
  • Verifiable client references: Contactable business owners who can speak to reliability and communication

The SEO Metrics Hierarchy: Vanity vs. Revenue

A common trap is accepting top-of-funnel vanity metrics as proof of business health:

Impressions & Visibility → Clicks & Traffic → Raw Inquiries → Qualified Leads → Revenue & Bookings

An agency reporting rankings alone has not necessarily added a single rupee to your business. A strong agency ties its reporting to the leads and revenue levels, not just visibility.

What Does Professional SEO Actually Include?

A capable SEO company should offer work across these five layers, not just one:

1. Technical SEO

Improves how search engines crawl, interpret, and index a website: crawling and indexation checks, XML sitemaps, robots.txt review, canonical tags, redirect management, mobile usability, page-speed improvements, Core Web Vitals, and structured data. Google's own SEO documentation recommends keeping sites accessible to search engines, using descriptive titles, and monitoring performance through Search Console.

2. On-Page SEO

Improves individual pages for both users and search engines: search-intent research, title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal links, image optimization, content improvements, and FAQ sections.

3. Local SEO

Critical for businesses serving specific locations: Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword research, location landing pages, NAP consistency, local citations, review generation, and map visibility. A Dehradun clinic, for example, might target terms like "dentist near Rajpur Road" or "emergency dental clinic in Dehradun," while a hotel might target "business hotel near ISBT Dehradun."

4. Content SEO

Publishing genuinely helpful resources matching what customers actually search for: service pages, location pages, comparison articles, buying guides, case studies, and FAQs. Avoid an agency that produces large volumes of generic articles without originality, accuracy, or a real connection to your products.

5. Off-Page SEO and Digital Authority

Digital PR, relevant industry listings, editorial mentions, local partnerships, and ethical link acquisition. Ask specifically how an agency acquires links: be cautious of any company promising hundreds of backlinks monthly without explaining relevance or risk.

Account Ownership: What You Must Retain

The client should always retain direct ownership of: Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Business Profile, website hosting, the domain registrar, and the website CMS. Never allow an agency to create these essential accounts using only its own email address: if the relationship ends, you should walk away with your full historical data intact, not start from zero with a new provider.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring an SEO Company

  1. What type of SEO does my business actually need?
  2. Have you worked with businesses in my industry?
  3. Can you show relevant, dated case studies?
  4. What will you do during the first 90 days?

  7. How will you measure leads and conversions, not just rankings?

  9. What happens if I end the contract?

Red Flags to Avoid

  • Promises like "number one on Google in seven days" or "guaranteed first position"
  • Reporting that shows only rankings, never business results
  • Full payment demanded upfront for a long contract with no exit clause
  • The agency owns your domain or ad accounts instead of you
  • No technical audit or clear strategy before signing
  • Generic monthly reports with no explanation of what changed or why

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best SEO company in Dehradun?

There is no independently verified universal number-one SEO company for every business. For documented, technical case-study results, agencies like Redsan Digitals show measurable ranking and CTR movement; for market tenure, firms citing 10-plus years bring longer local presence. Evaluate based on relevant case studies and transparency, not star ratings.

How much does SEO cost in Dehradun?

SEO services generally range from ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per month for basic local SEO up to ₹75,000 to ₹2,00,000-plus per month for competitive, national, or e-commerce SEO campaigns. Content, technical work, and taxes may be billed separately.

How long does SEO take to produce results?

For an established website, technical fixes and on-page optimization often show visibility improvements within 60 to 90 days. For new websites or highly competitive keywords, sustainable growth typically takes 4 to 8 months of consistent work.

Is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Yes. Local SEO places greater emphasis on Google Business Profile, maps, reviews, local citations, and searches with city or near me intent, compared to broader national SEO strategy.

Can an SEO company guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical agency should guarantee a specific Google ranking. Search results depend on competition, algorithm changes, website quality, and factors outside any agency's direct control; an agency can set realistic targets, not guarantees.
