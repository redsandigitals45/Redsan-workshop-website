# How Much Does a Business Website Cost in Dehradun in 2026?

## Quick Answer

A business website in Dehradun typically costs between **₹10,000 and ₹1,50,000**, depending on what the site actually needs to do. A basic 5–8 page presence site sits at the lower end. A lead-generating business website with a custom admin panel, the kind most Dehradun SMEs actually need, usually lands in the ₹30,000–₹80,000 range. If you're building a web application rather than a website (booking systems, dashboards, custom platforms), expect the price to move well past ₹1.5 lakh. Domain and hosting are **not included** in any of these numbers. More on why below.

That's the honest range for Dehradun's market. Not the inflated metro-agency numbers, and not the "we'll do it for 8k" freelancer numbers either, both of which cause problems we've seen firsthand.

## Why Dehradun Pricing Doesn't Match the National Averages

Most cost guides you'll find online quote national figures pulled from Bangalore or Mumbai agency rate cards. Dehradun clients don't have that pocket size, and honestly, most Dehradun businesses don't need what those numbers are pricing in either.

Dehradun is still growing its IT industry, and a lot of skilled talent still moves out to metro cities, which naturally pulls down the average quality and pricing you see locally today. That's shifting though. Dehradun is actively promoting startups now, and as that ecosystem matures, expect this pricing curve to move up steadily over the next few years. Right now, businesses here are getting a genuinely lower entry point than metro cities, but that window won't stay this wide forever.

The real determinant isn't the city, it's **what the website is actually supposed to do**. A brochure site and a lead-generation system with an admin panel are not the same product, even if they look similar on the front end. That's the single biggest thing we've seen Dehradun business owners get wrong when comparing quotes: they're comparing a ₹10,000 template against a ₹60,000 custom build without understanding they're not buying the same thing.

## What ₹35,000 Actually Bought: A Real Estate Case Study

We built [Grihya](https://grihya.in/), a real estate listings platform for a client operating across both Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Dehradun, on a ₹35,000 budget, covering roughly 8–12 pages **plus a custom admin panel** for managing listings.

That budget was tight for what the project actually required. Building listing management, an admin backend, and a functional front end for two operating regions on ₹35,000 is not a "template and go" job. It's closer to a lead-gen site with a light web-application layer bolted on. It worked because the scope was defined honestly upfront, not because ₹35,000 is what admin-panel builds "should" cost.

The tricky part wasn't the front end, it was getting the admin panel and the live listings to actually talk to each other cleanly, so a non-technical team member could manage two regions' worth of properties without breaking anything. That backend wiring is where most budget quietly disappears on a project like this. It's not glamorous work, but it's the difference between a website that looks like it has an admin panel and one that actually functions like a working system.

**The takeaway for readers:** if a Dehradun business owner is quoted ₹35,000 for something with real backend functionality, that's not automatically a red flag, but it means the scope needs to be watched closely, because there's very little room for "just one more feature."

## The Cost Nobody Quotes You Upfront: Domain & Hosting

One factor that should **always** sit outside your website build quote: domain and hosting.

Both of these depend entirely on availability and the extension you choose (.in, .com, .co.in, etc.). They're not part of what a developer or agency builds, and any quote that bundles them in without breaking out the cost separately is worth double-checking. Budget these as their own recurring line item, typically ₹500–₹15,000+ per year depending on your hosting needs, completely separate from the one-time build cost.

## FAQs

**Q1: Why do website costs vary so much between agencies in Dehradun?**
Pricing depends on scope, not just page count. A static 8-page brochure site and an 8-page site with an admin panel and backend functionality require completely different amounts of development work, even though they look similar from the outside.

**Q2: Should domain and hosting be included in my website quote?**
No. Domain and hosting costs depend on availability and extension, and are billed separately as recurring annual costs. A quote that bundles these into the one-time build cost makes it harder to compare apples-to-apples against other quotes.

**Q3: Is a cheaper website ever actually the more expensive option?**
Often, yes. A poorly built, template-based site with no SEO foundation frequently ends up needing a full rebuild later, meaning the business pays twice: once for a site that doesn't perform, and again to fix it properly.

**Q4: Do I need to budget for SEO separately from the website build?**
Foundational, on-page SEO should be part of the build itself, not an afterthought. Ongoing SEO (content, backlinks, rankings work) is a separate, recurring investment, but a site built without SEO fundamentals in place from day one will struggle to rank no matter how much you spend on SEO later.
