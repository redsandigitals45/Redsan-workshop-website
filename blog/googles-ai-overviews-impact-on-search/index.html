Google's AI Overviews: What the New Search Reality Means for Indian Businesses

RS
RedSan Growth Team
26 Aug 2026 · 5 min read

The rollout of Google's AI Overviews across India marks the most significant structural change to search engine architecture since the introduction of the knowledge graph. For years, publishing informational content was a reliable way to accumulate organic impressions and top-of-funnel web traffic.

Today, when users enter informational queries, Google's Gemini models synthesize answers directly at the top of the search engine results page (SERP). The user reads the AI answer and leaves without ever clicking an external link. This phenomenon: known in the industry as the Zero-Click Search Economy: demands an immediate strategic realignment for Indian brands.

Which Industries Are Most Impacted?

Data from recent algorithm tracking reveals varying degrees of disruption across commercial verticals:

  • Informational & Definitions (High Impact): Content answering "What is..." or basic factual questions has experienced up to a 45% reduction in organic click-through rates. AI answers these queries instantaneously.
  • High-Intent Commercial Queries (Moderate Impact): Searches for local services ("best corporate law firm Delhi", "digital marketing agency Dehradun") still trigger local packs, customer reviews, and direct agency listings. Buyers do not trust AI alone to pick a business partner.
  • Transactional & E-Commerce (Evolving Impact): Google is integrating merchant feeds directly into AI Overviews, displaying product carousels with real-time pricing and stock availability.
"The era of lazy, surface-level blog posts is dead. If an AI can answer your customer's query in two sentences, you never had a defensible content strategy to begin with."

The RedSan Take: How Indian Businesses Win in the AI Era

Rather than despairing over lost zero-click traffic, forward-thinking brands are turning AI Overviews into a dominant customer acquisition channel:

  1. Win the AI Citation Box: AI Overviews do not invent facts from thin air: they cite 3 to 5 authoritative source links in interactive carousels. Being one of those cited sources confers immense authority and drives high-intent referral traffic.
  2. Focus on Opinion, Proprietary Data & Field Studies: AI models cannot replicate original experiments, client case studies, verified pricing data, and first-hand founder perspectives. Content with high information gain is prioritized for citation.
  3. Dominate Bottom-of-Funnel Commercial Terms: Shift content investment away from generic top-of-funnel definitions and toward high-intent comparison queries: pricing teardowns, vendor alternatives, implementation frameworks, and local service pages.
Action Step: Audit your top 20 traffic-driving keywords in Google Search Console. Identify which queries trigger AI Overviews and check whether your brand is cited. If not, structure concise direct-answer blocks (40-60 words) immediately beneath matching H2 headers.

Summary: The Future Belongs to Verified Authority

Google's AI Overviews are not the end of SEO: they are the beginning of generative authority. Brands that combine technical speed, schema markup, and authentic primary insights will capture an outsized share of market attention.
