Redsan's Dehradun Chapter: How It All Began in 2023

SR
Sankalp Reddy
14 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

01. Chandigarh → 02. Dehradun → 03. International → 04. Founder → 05. Redsan Today → 06. What's Next
Quick Answer: Redsan moved to Dehradun in 2023 after its Chandigarh chapter, getting its start through incubation at Uttaranchal University under the guidance of Dr. Rajesh Singh and Dr. Anita Gehlot. From there, the agency grew its Dehradun client base across fitness, cafes, multi-location restaurants, and schools. Along the way, early operational friction forced Redsan to institute formal standard operating procedures (SOPs) and specialized delegation: turning hard-learned lessons into the foundation of today's agency engine.

2023: Establishing Roots in Dehradun

After operating out of Chandigarh and Kharar, the vision for Redsan demanded a permanent home: a city where we could build our headquarters, assemble a multidisciplinary core team, and participate directly in an emerging commercial ecosystem.

In 2023, Redsan established its base in Dehradun. The first institutional relationship set the foundation for everything that followed.

First Anchor: Incubation at Uttaranchal University

Redsan's Dehradun journey officially began when the agency was accepted for startup incubation at Uttaranchal University, under the dedicated mentorship of Dr. Rajesh Singh and Dr. Anita Gehlot. This was far more than an ordinary vendor engagement: it provided institutional credibility, workspace infrastructure, and academic mentorship that grounded the agency in the region.

Redsan partnered with Uttaranchal University on student-centric content campaigns, growing the university's official page following from 8,500 to over 10,000 active members.

Building UIM's Instagram From Scratch

Working alongside faculty mentors and student peers at the Uttaranchal Institute of Management, Redsan helped establish and launch the official UIM Instagram handle. Redsan's founder personally filmed, edited, and published the very first reel on that profile. Today, that community has grown past 11,000 followers and continues to be driven forward by successive cohorts of student contributors.

The channel was later managed by Krrish Maindola, who was outsourced as an expert to assist the Redsan team while operating his own agency work, and has since established his own digital marketing company, Digitalurlife.

Expanding Into Dehradun's Business Landscape

With university credibility backing our execution, Redsan rapidly began partnering with commercial brands across Dehradun's fitness, culinary, and retail sectors.

  • › Golds Gym Dehradun: Continuing our fitness track record from Kharar, Redsan produced on-location workout and culture reels for Golds Gym Dehradun (Reel 01 and Reel 02).
  • › Elloras Melting Moments: Collaborated on high-visual promotional video reels for one of Dehradun's most celebrated heritage bakery brands.
  • › Turkish Dastarkhwan Restaurant: Handled end-to-end digital media strategy and content creation as the restaurant scaled across two distinct branches: one near Nanda ki Chowki, and the second near the UPES Bidholi campus. (Instagram Profile)
  • › Amnesia Cafe Dehradun: Directed social creative campaigns for Amnesia Cafe, creating student-friendly nightlife and dining promotions.

The Roasted Culture Group: SIORA, Gunpowder & Meeting Real Talent

Redsan's food and beverage work expanded further when we partnered with the Roasted Culture restaurant collective, beginning with SIORA by Roasted Culture (@siorabyroastedculture).

This engagement created memorable moments for our young production crew: it brought the team into direct collaboration with veteran actor Mr. Manish Wadhwa (widely acclaimed for his iconic portrayal of Chanakya). Additionally, our team edited and produced video content featuring reviews from other recognizable figures, including actor-comedian Faruqui Lilliput and Bollywood playback singer Shahid Mallya.

Following SIORA, Redsan went on to manage social media deliverables for Gunpowder by RC (@gunpowderbyrc) and separately partnered with OG-Chaap (@ogchaap), expanding our operational footprint across Dehradun's culinary corridor.

The Hard Lesson: Growing Pains and the Birth of Real SOPs

Not every chapter in an agency's story is a smooth victory, and it is important to be completely transparent about that. Taking on multiple fast-moving restaurants and cafes simultaneously pushed our early production capacity to its limits.

While the creative output and visual aesthetics were consistently strong, our operational processes were still immature. Deliverables experienced delays, technical miscommunications occurred, and we received fair, critical feedback from clients regarding turnaround times.

The Turning Point in Agency Operations: Those painful reviews were the best thing that could have happened to Redsan. Instead of making excuses, we rebuilt our entire workflow from scratch. We developed clear Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), established explicit approval queues, and stopped assigning generalists to juggle five different disciplines at once. The reliability and turnaround discipline our clients experience today was forged directly out of those mistakes.
The Early Crew Who Built the Foundation

None of our subsequent growth would have been possible without the core teammates who showed up every single day during that demanding period without complaint: Tushar, Ajay Raj Negi, Animesh Dabral, Rohit Negi, Hussain Ahmed, and Umar Gaur, alongside expert outsourced support from Krrish Maindola. Acknowledging the people who stood shoulder to shoulder during the hard, imperfect days matters far more than listing vanity client logos.

Expanding Into Education: The Gyanada International School

During this same phase, Redsan expanded outside the hospitality and fitness realm by taking on digital marketing for The Gyanada International School. Managing an educational institution required a completely different tone: transitioning from high-energy culinary reels to thoughtful, community-focused parent communication, student achievements, and admissions storytelling.

What Came Next

The early 2023 Dehradun period was our proving ground. It tested our resilience, taught us how to manage team bandwidth under pressure, and proved that institutional backing combined with relentless execution could plant deep roots in a competitive regional capital.

From these early cafe and campus roots, Redsan evolved into full-funnel digital infrastructure: scaling into custom web engineering, technical SEO, performance advertising, and regional enterprise retainers across North India.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did Redsan Digitals move to Dehradun?

Redsan moved to Dehradun in 2023, after operating out of Chandigarh, and got its start through a startup incubation at Uttaranchal University.

What was Redsan's first Dehradun client relationship?

Uttaranchal University, where Redsan was incubated as a startup under the guidance of Dr. Rajesh Singh and Dr. Anita Gehlot, running student-centric content campaigns that grew the university's social following past 10,000 followers.

What kind of clients did Redsan work with early on in Dehradun?

A gym (Golds Gym Dehradun), several cafes and restaurants (including Turkish Dastarkhwan, Amnesia Cafe, and the Roasted Culture group with SIORA and Gunpowder), Elloras Melting Moments, and The Gyanada International School.

Did everything go smoothly during this early Dehradun phase?

No, and Redsan is candid about that. Technical process errors during a cluster of cafe and restaurant projects led to delays and critical feedback. That experience directly led to building proper standard operating procedures (SOPs) and disciplined task delegation.
