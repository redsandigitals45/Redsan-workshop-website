# Redsan's Dehradun Chapter: How It All Began in 2023

## Quick Answer

Redsan moved to Dehradun in 2023 after its Chandigarh chapter, and got its start by being incubated at Uttaranchal University. From there, the agency grew its Dehradun client base across cafes, restaurants, a gym, and a school, learning some hard operational lessons along the way that shaped how Redsan runs client work today.

---

## 2023: Establishing Roots in Dehradun

After working in Chandigarh, it was time to establish Redsan properly in Dehradun. The first client relationship set the foundation, quite literally, for the agency's next chapter.

---

## First Client: Uttaranchal University

Redsan's Dehradun story began with getting incubated as a startup at [Uttaranchal University](https://uudoon.in/research/startups-and-enterpreneurship.php), under the guidance of Dr. Rajesh Singh and Dr. Anita Gehlot. This wasn't just a client relationship, it was institutional backing that gave the agency real footing in a new city.

Redsan worked with Uttaranchal University on student-centric content campaigns, growing the university's page followers from 8,500 to over 10,000.

### Building UIM's Instagram From Scratch

Alongside some of the best faculty at Uttaranchal University and a group of good friends and colleagues at the Uttaranchal Institute of Management, Redsan helped establish and found [the UIM Instagram handle](https://www.instagram.com/official_uim/), now grown to more than 11,000 followers and currently run by the next generation of student contributors. Redsan's founder personally recorded and uploaded [the very first video](https://www.instagram.com/p/DBvBV_GvN8V/) on that page.

The account was later handled by [Krrish Maindola](https://www.linkedin.com/in/krrishmaindola/), who has since gone on to run his own digital marketing agency, [Digitalurlife](https://digitalurlife.com/).

---

## Expanding Into Dehradun's Business Scene

With institutional credibility established, Redsan began picking up clients across Dehradun's growing business landscape.

**Golds Gym Dehradun** became one of the early clients, with Redsan producing gym promotional videos ([one](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Cx2Bh99ydBH/?stkn=MTEyd2RpN3BpNW84Zw==), [two](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CytNmrmylN4/?stkn=MXJ2OXdqZ3NvempneA==)).

**Elloras Melting Moments** followed as another client, with Redsan producing [promotional video content](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C2KbAMxyc2H/?stkn=MTZrOWpnaHV5dWFnag==) for the brand.

**Turkish Dastarkhwan Restaurant** became one of Redsan's more prominent Dehradun clients, with the agency preparing content and managing the full social media presence as the business expanded to two locations, one near Nanda ki Chowki, and another near the UPES campus.

Redsan also worked with **Amnesia Cafe**, another Dehradun food and beverage client.

---

## The Roasted Culture Group: SIORA, Gunpowder, and Meeting Real Talent

Redsan's work extended into the Roasted Culture group of restaurants, starting with **SIORA by Roasted Culture**. This engagement stood out for an unexpected reason: it brought the Redsan team into direct contact with Manish Wadhwa, the actor renowned for his portrayal of Chanakya. The team also edited video content featuring reviews from other well-known names, including comedian [Faruqui Lilliput](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaK3yv9ygXh/) and singer Shahid Mallya.

Redsan went on to work with **Gunpowder by RC**, another restaurant under the same group, and separately with **OG-Chaap**, continuing the agency's growing footprint in Dehradun's food and beverage scene.

---

## The Hard Lesson: Growing Pains in the Cafe and Restaurant Work

Not everything in this phase went smoothly, and it's worth being direct about that. Working with this cluster of cafes and restaurants was a genuine learning experience. The content itself was strong, but the team, still building its internal processes at the time, made technical errors that delayed client work. That led to some fair criticism and a few less-than-ideal reviews of Redsan's service during this period.

That period is exactly what pushed the agency to build real standard operating procedures and get more deliberate about assigning work to people with the right specific skills, rather than spreading a small team thin across too many deliverables at once. The Redsan team operating today is more productive and more disciplined specifically because of what went wrong during this phase, not in spite of it.

None of that growth would have been possible without the people who stuck with it through the hard parts. This phase of Redsan was built by a team that worked hard and never complained through the whole journey: Tushar, Ajay Raj Negi, Animesh Dabral, Krrish Maindola, Rohit Negi, Hussain Ahmed, and Umar Gaur. That kind of loyalty during a genuinely difficult growth period is worth naming, not just the client wins.

## Education Sector: The Gyanada School

Redsan also handled content and social media for [The Gyanada International School](https://www.instagram.com/thegyanadaschool/) during this same early period, extending the agency's work beyond food, fitness, and higher education into K-12 school marketing.

---

## Just the Beginning

This was the early phase of Redsan building its presence in Dehradun. From here, the agency expanded quickly into a much wider and more established client base, a phase with its own story to tell.

## FAQs

**Q1: When did Redsan Digitals move to Dehradun?**
Redsan moved to Dehradun in 2023, after operating out of Chandigarh, and got its start through a startup incubation at Uttaranchal University.

**Q2: What was Redsan's first Dehradun client relationship?**
Uttaranchal University, where Redsan was incubated as a startup under the guidance of Dr. Rajesh Singh and Dr. Anita Gehlot, and ran student-centric content campaigns that grew the university's social following past 10,000 followers.

**Q3: What kind of clients did Redsan work with early on in Dehradun?**
A gym, several cafes and restaurants (including a multi-location restaurant and a group of restaurants under Roasted Culture), and a K-12 school, alongside continued work with Uttaranchal University and its management institute.

**Q4: Did everything go smoothly during this early Dehradun phase?**
No, and Redsan doesn't hide that. Technical process errors during a cluster of cafe and restaurant projects led to delays and some negative feedback at the time. That experience directly led to building proper SOPs and better task assignment, which shaped how the agency operates today.
