# Redsan's Chandigarh Story: Before Dehradun, There Was Kharar

## Quick Answer

Before establishing its base in Dehradun, Redsan Digitals started as a small independent agency in Chandigarh in November 2020. The first client was a neighbourhood cafe in Kharar, Punjab, and within a year Redsan was managing digital marketing for an entire business group spanning a gym, a mall, a resort, a hotel, and a clothing brand. Redsan still retains many of these Chandigarh-era clients today and remains open to new work in the region, even while its current focus has shifted to Dehradun.

---

## November 2020: Starting Independent in Chandigarh

Redsan began operating as a small independent agency out of Chandigarh in November 2020. Like most young agencies, the first real client relationship set the tone for everything that followed.

---

## The First Client: FunBuzz Cafe, Kharar

[FunBuzz](https://www.instagram.com/funbuzz_kharar_/) was a friendly neighbourhood cafe in Kharar, Punjab, run by its owner, Harry. Redsan handled the cafe's full social media presence, producing more than 150 posts over a retention period of nearly two years.

During this engagement, the team was joined by Ajay Karki, [an exceptional Nepal-based dance performer and content creator](https://www.instagram.com/ajthecreator___/), who helped produce videos that used the cafe itself as a dance backdrop to promote the space. Redsan's founder joined some of these videos directly for fun, [community-style content](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cq7Nl_psnSA/) that built genuine local engagement rather than polished, distant advertising.

---

## The Shivjot Builders Era: From One Client to an Entire Group

The quality of work at FunBuzz got noticed in the local market. [Shivjot Builders](https://shivjotbuilders.com/) reached out and became one of Redsan's biggest clients at the time, expanding the relationship into promoting multiple businesses under the same organization.

### Golds Gym → Wolf Gym → LevelUp Gym, Kharar

Redsan produced high-quality gym videos and creatives for what started as Golds Gym Kharar, later rebranded to Wolf Gym Kharar, and eventually settled as [LevelUp Gym Kharar](https://www.instagram.com/levelup_gyms/reels/). During this period, Redsan's founder personally took on the role of marketing manager, handling day-to-day operations, while Ajay Karki worked as a professional dance coach at the gym itself.

### Becoming the Primary Agency for the Group

As the relationship deepened, Redsan became the primary digital marketing agency for the entire Shivjot group, and the founder took on the marketing manager role across several of its businesses at once:

- **Shivjot Mall 24/7**, where team member [Amarnath](https://www.instagram.com/amarhoonmai/) joined Redsan and helped both create content and manage store operations from within. ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mall_shivjot24/))
- **Urban Clothes**, the group's clothing brand.
- **Shivjot Resorts**, with full social media and content management. ([Website](https://shivjotresorts.com/) / [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shivjotresorts/))
- **Shivjot Hotel**, managed alongside the rest of the group's digital presence.

Some of the promotional work during this period put the founder directly on camera, including [this](https://www.instagram.com/p/Csjo5xbBg3w/) and [this](https://www.instagram.com/p/Crkl_DEMIys/) promotional video. Redsan worked with the Shivjot group for close to a year before the team's focus moved to Dehradun.

---

## An International Client, Even From Chandigarh: Prep Germany

While based in Chandigarh, Redsan also worked with Prep Germany, a Germany-based client focused on helping people prepare for jobs in Germany. Redsan produced content and managed the organization's social media account throughout this engagement, an early signal that the agency's client base was never going to stay purely local.

---

## Community Work: Open Mics and Local Business Campaigns

Beyond client accounts, Redsan's founder was also directly involved in organizing promotional events in Chandigarh, including open mic nights alongside the Lazzate Alfaz team, founded by [Amarnath](https://www.instagram.com/amarhoonmai/), who later joined the Redsan team. Amarnath is regarded as one of the finest poets to come out of Chandigarh's spoken word scene, with performances appraised by Social Club in Sector 7, Chandigarh, [including sets alongside stand-up comedian Vishal Tyagi](https://www.instagram.com/p/CnLxfeXP6l2/).

On the business side, Redsan also ran Meta ad campaigns for local food businesses, including First Bite Pizza and a local Indian Dhaba, focused on driving online orders. That work scaled across five different locations at the time, managed through multiple digital channels simultaneously.

Another standout from this period: [Momopur](https://www.instagram.com/momopur_official/), which started as a small street-style cafe. Redsan created and managed the brand's Instagram account from scratch, and Momopur has since grown into a multi-franchise establishment across Punjab. Building a brand from the ground up, rather than just managing an existing one, is core to how Redsan approaches client work.

---

## Chandigarh Today: Still Active, No Longer the Focus

Redsan still conducts business in Chandigarh, though the agency isn't actively expanding there while its current focus remains Dehradun. Most of the client relationships built during this era are still retained today.

One relationship led somewhere unexpected: Harry, the original FunBuzz owner, now resides in Canada, and referred Redsan for online reputation management and digital PR work with [Yasin Banqueting Suite](https://yasinbanquetingsuite.co.uk/). That's Redsan's Chandigarh-era client relationships still generating new work years later, through direct referral rather than outbound sales.

Redsan remains open to working with Chandigarh-based clients today.

---

## Why This Chapter Matters

This period shows something that's harder to fake than a client logo wall: a small independent agency growing a single cafe relationship into managing an entire business group's digital presence, across a gym, a mall, a resort, a hotel, and a clothing brand, largely through word of mouth and results rather than outbound sales. That kind of organic growth, and the retention that followed it years later, is a track record, not a pitch.
