Redsan's Chandigarh Story: Before Dehradun, There Was Kharar

SR
Sankalp Reddy
14 Sep 2026 · 6 min read
Quick Answer: Before establishing its base in Dehradun, Redsan Digitals started as a small independent agency in Chandigarh in November 2020. The first client was a neighbourhood cafe in Kharar, Punjab, and within a year Redsan was managing digital marketing for an entire business group spanning a gym, a mall, a resort, a hotel, and a clothing brand. Redsan still retains many of these Chandigarh-era clients today and remains open to new work in the region, even while its current focus has shifted to Dehradun.

November 2020: Starting Independent in Chandigarh

Redsan began operating as a small independent agency out of Chandigarh in November 2020. Like most young agencies, the first real client relationship set the tone for everything that followed.

Rather than launching with aggressive pitch decks or paid cold ads, the early foundation was built entirely on ground-level creative output, local community presence, and hands-on digital media management across the Tri-City and Kharar belt.

The First Client: FunBuzz Cafe, Kharar

FunBuzz was a friendly neighbourhood cafe in Kharar, Punjab, run by its owner, Harry. Redsan handled the cafe's full social media presence, producing more than 150 posts over a retention period of nearly two years.

During this engagement, the team was joined by Ajay Karki, an exceptional Nepal-based dance performer and content creator, who helped produce videos that used the cafe itself as a dance backdrop to promote the space. Redsan's founder joined some of these videos directly for fun, community-style content that built genuine local engagement rather than polished, distant advertising.

FunBuzz Cafe

Kharar, Punjab · First Retainer Client
  • Over 150 dedicated creative posts and short-form video assets produced.
  • Close to 2 years of continuous retainer retention.
  • Viral on-location dance promotions featuring creator Ajay Karki and the Redsan founding team.
Key Takeaway: Proving that localized, high-energy content outperforms sterile corporate graphics for hospitality brands.

The Shivjot Builders Era: From One Client to an Entire Group

The quality of work at FunBuzz got noticed in the local market. Shivjot Builders reached out and became one of Redsan's biggest clients at the time, expanding the relationship into promoting multiple businesses under the same organization.

Golds Gym → Wolf Gym → LevelUp Gym, Kharar

Redsan produced high-quality gym videos and creatives for what started as Golds Gym Kharar, later rebranded to Wolf Gym Kharar, and eventually settled as LevelUp Gym Kharar. During this period, Redsan's founder personally took on the role of marketing manager, handling day-to-day operations, while Ajay Karki worked as a professional dance coach at the gym itself.

Becoming the Primary Agency for the Group

As the relationship deepened, Redsan became the primary digital marketing agency for the entire Shivjot group, and the founder took on the marketing manager role across several of its businesses at once:

  • › Shivjot Mall 24/7: Team member Amarnath joined Redsan and helped both create content and manage store operations from within. (Instagram Profile)
  • › Urban Clothes: The group's retail clothing brand, managing visual cataloguing and social promotions.
  • › Shivjot Resorts: Full social media, hospitality visual identity, and content management. (Website / Instagram)
  • › Shivjot Hotel: Managed alongside the rest of the group's digital presence and booking discovery.

Some of the promotional work during this period put the founder directly on camera, including this promo and this on-location video. Redsan worked with the Shivjot group for close to a year before the team's primary expansion moved toward Dehradun.

An International Client, Even From Chandigarh: Prep Germany

While based in Chandigarh, Redsan also worked with Prep Germany, a Germany-based client focused on helping students and professionals prepare for jobs and relocation in Germany. Redsan produced targeted content and managed the organization's social media accounts throughout this engagement: an early signal that the agency's client base was never going to stay purely regional.

Community Work: Open Mics and Local Business Campaigns

Beyond client accounts, Redsan's founder was directly involved in organizing promotional events and community gatherings in Chandigarh. This included organizing open mic nights alongside the Lazzate Alfaz team, founded by Amarnath, who later joined the core Redsan team. Amarnath is regarded as one of the finest poets to emerge from Chandigarh's spoken word scene, with recognized performances appraised by Social Club in Sector 7, Chandigarh, including sets alongside stand-up comedian Vishal Tyagi.

On the paid performance side, Redsan also ran Meta ad campaigns for local food businesses, including First Bite Pizza and a popular local Indian Dhaba, focused specifically on driving online orders. That work scaled across five different locations simultaneously, managed through multi-channel digital funnels.

Momopur: From Ground Zero to Multi-Franchise Brand

Punjab Brand Incubation

Another major standout from this period is Momopur, which originated as a small street-style cafe. Redsan created and managed the brand's Instagram presence and visual narrative from absolute scratch.

Scale Milestone: Momopur has since expanded into a flourishing multi-franchise food brand across Punjab. Building a brand from zero, rather than merely maintaining an established account, sits at the heart of Redsan's strategic playbook.

Chandigarh Today: Still Active, No Longer the Sole Focus

Redsan still conducts active business in Chandigarh, though the agency is not actively deploying aggressive outbound sales in the city while current expansion remains centered on Dehradun and the National Capital Region. Most client relationships built during this formative era remain active today.

One early relationship led somewhere entirely unexpected: Harry, the original FunBuzz owner, subsequently relocated to Canada and later referred Redsan for online reputation management and digital PR work with Yasin Banqueting Suite in the United Kingdom. That is Redsan's Chandigarh-era relationship base generating international client engagements years later, through organic referral trust rather than outbound cold pitches.

Redsan remains fully open to partnering with ambitious Chandigarh and Tri-City businesses seeking world-class digital infrastructure, content, and revenue performance.

Why This Chapter Matters

This period demonstrates something that is impossible to fabricate with surface-level marketing: a young independent agency expanding a single cafe account into managing digital marketing across an entire business conglomerate: gym, shopping mall, resort, hotel, and apparel retail: driven entirely by word-of-mouth recommendations and verifiable business results.

That kind of grassroots organic traction, and the multi-year client retention that continues to this day, represents a verifiable track record of execution excellence.
