Service Pricing & Policy Guide

Base Rate Card for Dehradun & Uttarakhand : Commercial Policy Guide
Effective Date: 16 September 2026
Last Reviewed: 16 September 2026
Tax Standard: Exclusive of 18% GST
Download PDF (Official Copy)

This Service Pricing & Policy Guide (“Guide”) sets out Redsan Digitals Pvt. Ltd.’s base pricing for its core services, benchmarked against current Dehradun and wider Tier-2 India market rates, along with the policies that govern how pricing is set, quoted, and revised. Figures below are starting and indicative rates for typical small-to-mid-sized local and regional clients; every engagement is ultimately scoped and quoted via a signed proposal or Statement of Work (SOW) as described in the Master Service Agreement.

Transparent Commercial Policy: We deliver enterprise-grade performance marketing and engineering while maintaining Tier-2 regional operational efficiency: offering rates approximately 20% to 40% below metro agencies without sacrificing technical craftsmanship.

1. Pricing Philosophy

  • Base pricing reflects Dehradun and Uttarakhand regional market conditions: generally 20% to 40% below metro-city (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru) agency rates for comparable technical and creative scope;
  • All monthly packages are starting prices for a single brand or location with standard scope; multi-location, multi-brand, or high-competition engagements are quoted individually;
  • All stated fees are exclusive of GST (currently 18%), which is added at the prevailing statutory rate on every invoice;
  • Advertising spend (Google, Meta, LinkedIn media budgets) and third-party pass-through costs (domain registration, cloud hosting, software licenses, stock media) are always billed separately from agency fees; and
  • Every engagement is confirmed via a signed proposal or SOW under the Master Service Agreement before work begins; rates in this Guide serve as an indicative baseline until confirmed in writing.

9. What Is Included & Excluded Across All Packages

Included by Default: Strategic planning, disciplined execution, agreed deliverable revision rounds, transparent performance analytics, and a designated project point of contact.

Excluded by Default: Media ad budgets paid to Google/Meta, cloud hosting and domain fees, third-party software subscriptions, stock asset licenses, paid API keys, and work outside the signed scope (which is billed via a Change Order).

10. Custom & Bespoke Pricing

We tailor quotes for multi-location brands, competitive high-CPC industries (e.g., real estate, finance, healthcare), multi-lingual campaigns, and rush turnarounds (which incur a 20% to 50% expedited fee). Bundled multi-channel retainers (SEO + SMM + Ads) receive an automatic 10% to 15% package discount.

11. Payment Terms

In accordance with Section 5 of our Master Service Agreement: one-time project milestones are billed in installments; monthly retainer invoices are payable in advance of each billing cycle; invoices carry a 7-day payment window; and statutory taxes are added at invoice generation.

13. Price Revision Policy

Base rates are evaluated annually against regional market conditions. For active retainer clients, any fee adjustment is communicated at least 30 days in advance and will not apply to an active committed billing term.
