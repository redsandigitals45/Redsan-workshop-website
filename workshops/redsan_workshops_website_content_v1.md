# 22. CONTENT PRINCIPLES

Every future Redsan Workshop page should follow these principles:

### 1. Practical over theoretical

Show what the learner will build.

### 2. Outcomes over feature lists

Explain what the learner can actually do after learning.

### 3. Real experience over exaggerated claims

Use verified projects, experience and testimonials.

### 4. AI as a multiplier

Do not position AI as a substitute for fundamental marketing knowledge.

### 5. Student-first language

Speak to the problems and ambitions of students and early-stage builders.

### 6. No unrealistic income promises

Never guarantee earnings, clients, jobs or business outcomes.

### 7. Keep the first product simple

One flagship workshop first.

Validate demand.

Then expand.
